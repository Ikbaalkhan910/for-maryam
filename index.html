A little reminder, sweetheart

Please talk to me. You don't have to hide your fears. You don't have to apologize for needing comfort.

I cannot promise that I'll always know the perfect words, but I can promise to listen, to be honest, and to keep trying to understand you.

My darling, breathe for a moment. You don't have to figure everything out right now. One difficult thought doesn't define our relationship. One difficult day doesn't erase the care between us. You deserve patience, gentleness and reassurance. I'm here because I want to be here. ♡

And when we misunderstand each other, I want us to remember that we're on the same team.

Maybe then you'd understand how precious your kindness is, how beautiful your little habits are, and how much you deserve a love that doesn't make you question your worth.
