My Princess Maryam

If the stars could speak, they would tell you what I see in you.

If someone ever asked me what makes a person beautiful, I wouldn't know where to begin. Because then I'd think of you, and suddenly every ordinary description would feel too small.

Those beautiful eyes of yours... I wonder what kind of magic they hold. Every time I look at them, the world seems to grow quieter. For a moment, I forget everything around me. It's as if the stars decided to settle in your eyes instead of the sky.

And your smile? Oh, your smile. I don't think you realise how easily it changes my day. Even on the hardest days, the thought of seeing you smile is enough to make my heart feel lighter.

But what makes you truly special isn't just your eyes or your smile. It's your heart. The way you ask whether I've eaten, whether I've had enough water, and whether I'm taking care of myself. Little things, perhaps, but to me they mean more than you know.

I admire the kindness in you. The way you try to understand me, the way you make time for me even when life gets busy, and the way you make ordinary moments feel like something worth remembering.

If I were a prince in a kingdom full of treasures, I would still consider myself lucky just to know someone like you. Because no crown, palace, or treasure could ever compare to the beauty of a heart as gentle as yours.

I don't need you to be perfect, Maryam. I just hope you never stop being the wonderful person you are. And if I could give you one thing, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes, even for a moment.

Maybe then you'd understand why I find you so magnificent.
